# Part I — English

## Abstract

Open-ended evolution (OEE) — the continued production of novel, ever more complex behaviour without convergence on a fixed goal — is a central open problem in artificial life. Almost all evolutionary computing systems rely on a designer-specified scalar fitness function to drive selection, whereas biological evolution has none. Meanwhile, LLM-based multi-agent social simulations face the "static sandbox" critique: predefined tasks, closed environments and rigid evaluation criteria suppress emergence and co-evolution. This paper reports an LLM multi-agent world simulator under development (the "Godtown" / 创世小镇 project). It replaces a scalar fitness function with six immutable physical theorems (Conservation, Causality, Time, Space, Cost, Finitude); it replaces predefined cultural types with a free-text *idea pool*; it provides a cross-generational *neurocore* whose weights, unlike an individual's memory, survive the death of its host; and, as of v4.9.0, it gives each resident a *subjective world model* (beliefs) and a rough *standing field*. The design principle is "physical substrate hardcoded, everything above the substrate fully open." Evidence reported here is engineering-level: 53 self-test stages (each stage a group of semantically related assertions) comprising 1431 assertions, all passing; 26 browser-level smoke checks; and a 400-day two-process determinism verification. We do not claim to have solved OEE; we report the design decisions and preliminary evidence of a system that can serve as a platform for empirical OEE research.

**Keywords:** open-ended evolution; fitness-free evolution; LLM multi-agent systems; artificial life; constraint-driven selection; cultural accumulation

## 1 Introduction

Biological evolution has sustained continuous innovation for billions of years with no fitness function. Since Bedau et al. (2000) framed open-endedness as an "open problem", the artificial life community has sought to reproduce this property in artificial systems. As the Genesis platform observes, however, "almost all evolutionary algorithms rely on a fitness function." Even recent neural cellular automata obtain self-replication and mutation under a predefined training loss rather than spontaneously in a goal-free environment.

At the same time, LLM-driven multi-agent simulations (e.g., Smallville, Project Sid) demonstrate the potential of social emergence, yet a 2025 position paper (Chen et al.) argues that "most current simulations remain trapped in static sandboxes — predefined tasks, limited dynamics, rigid evaluation criteria," and calls for a shift from task-specific optimisation to open-ended co-evolution.

This paper is an engineering response to that call. Its core principle: **the physical substrate is hardcoded; everything above the substrate is fully open.** Specifically:

1. **The location of the fitness function is taken by physical constraints.** The system defines no scalar reward. Six eternal theorems (Conservation, Causality, Time, Space, Cost, Finitude) play a role isomorphic to "constraint-driven selection" in Genesis: they state only what is impossible, never what should be pursued.
2. **The cultural layer has no predefined types.** An idea is free text, not a predefined category. Currency, religion and institutions emerge from the idea pool rather than being defined by the developer.
3. **The cross-generational evolutionary unit is independent of the individual.** The "neurocore" is a weight carrier that does not disappear when its host dies; it drifts, waiting for the next newborn. This is an architectural choice distinct from JaxLife's cross-generational cultural accumulation.
4. **(v4.9.0) Residents carry a subjective world model and a distributed standing field.** Each resident holds *beliefs* — propositions that may be wrong or stale — and each resident's social weight is computed from what others have witnessed, not from a job table.

## 2 Related Work

### 2.1 Fitness-free evolution
Genesis (Sharma, GECCO 2026) replaces scalar fitness with constraint-driven selection (CDS), constraint-adaptive regulation (CARP) and an artificial immune system (AIS); over more than a million generations it observed sustained evolutionary activity in 7/12 runs and produced a "sham-controlled" negative result showing that niche construction alone does not escape a complexity plateau. Our system shares the philosophical stance — constraints instead of fitness — but differs in scale: Genesis runs small agents on a Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion substrate, whereas our system runs a full social simulation with bodies, relationships, language, a material chain and institutions. Our "constraints" are therefore not one metabolic cost function but physical laws distributed across subsystems (matter must be carried; knowledge dies with its carrier; time is irreversible).

### 2.2 Open-ended agent simulators
JaxLife (Lu et al., ALIFE 2024) targets agents that accumulate open-ended culture and technology across generations, and reports basic communication protocols, agriculture and tool use. Its agents are parameterised by deep neural networks optimised under a predefined survival fitness. Our system differs in two ways: resident behaviour is produced by a hybrid of a rule-based utility AI and LLM decisions rather than by a neural network; and the selection pressure is the joint constraint of six theorems rather than a single survival signal — a resident may survive yet be unable to do what he "wants", because the material does not exist, the knowledge is lost, or space and time cannot be skipped.

### 2.3 LLM multi-agent and social simulation
The Chen et al. (2025) position paper criticises the "static sandbox" and proposes a roadmap centred on open-endedness, continuous co-evolution and resilient AI ecosystems. Our system responds to three of its criticisms directly: **predefined tasks** → the system has no tasks; residents are driven by needs, conflicts and ideas. **Closed environment** → the system is an infinite map, generated on demand, and those who leave continue to move in a reduced-precision simulation. **Rigid evaluation** → success is not task-completion rate but the persistence of evolutionary activity (rates of idea generation/spread/mutation/crystallisation, lineage diversity, relic retention).

### 2.4 Measuring open-endedness
Bedau's evolutionary activity statistics are the most widely used OEE metric; Genesis developed PNCT (Population-level Non-fitness Characterisation Toolkit) including the genetic activity coefficient (GAC) and expressed phenotypic complexity (EPC). Our system currently uses different measures (distribution of idea holders, crystallisation events, knowledge-loss rate, long-range determinism). Our metrics are not directly comparable to GAC/EPC, but they share the same goal: quantifying the persistence of evolutionary activity rather than task completion. Aligning them with GAC/EPC is future work, and we do not claim this alignment yet.

## 3 System Design

### 3.1 Design principle
The core judgement: **in a sufficiently complex simulation, predefining "what is worth evolving" is more dangerous than letting evolution decide "what is worth it."** The system therefore hardcodes only the physical layer and delegates all content to runtime mechanisms.

| Layer | Hardcoded? | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Yes | Six eternal theorems, material conservation, body anatomy, discrete time and space |
| Content | No | Recipes, language, customs, event templates, building composition, social roles, ideas |
| Meta | No | What counts as a "gap", what counts as "novel", what counts as "worth fixing" |

### 3.2 The six eternal theorems

| Theorem | Meaning | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Conservation | Matter is neither created nor destroyed | Material chain is conserved slot-by-slot (strict mode); combination/separation/transformation preserve totals |
| Causality | Every effect has a cause; it is irreversible | Every intent must carry a `reason` field; actions produce effects, effects enter memory |
| Time | One-way, cannot be reversed | Rollback is removed; abandoning a world destroys it permanently |
| Space | Discrete grid; no teleportation | Pathfinding, hauling and material transport must pass through intermediate cells |
| Cost | Every change has a cost | Stamina, time and material consumption; there are no free actions |
| Finitude | Resources, lifespan, energy are finite | Population caps, resource caps, knowledge carrier (a human brain) is finite |

These are isomorphic to Genesis's "physical gating" but are not expressed as a single metabolic cost function: conservation lives in the material system, causality in the intent contract, one-way time in the save philosophy, space in pathfinding, cost in the body system, and finitude in the population and knowledge systems.

### 3.3 The idea pool: untyped cultural evolution
An *idea* is free text up to 40 characters, with no predefined category. Each day a resident has a 1.2% chance to form an idea from recent events, an unresolved conflict, or a bodily sensation. The town-wide pool holds at most 48 ideas; a single resident holds at most 6 (the theorem of finitude). The life cycle:

- **Formation** — a free-text expression abstracted from the resident's current state;
- **Spread** — 8% chance per conversation; with 15% chance the idea mutates through a lexical mutator (" gossip drifts over three tellings");
- **Selection** — fitness is the holders' mean wellbeing (mood 40% + energy 30% + satiety 30%) over a sliding 7-day window;
- **Death** — when fitness < 0.3, a 2% daily chance to be forgotten;
- **Crystallisation** — when holders reach 30% of the adult population and fitness ≥ 0.45, the idea becomes a custom, carrying its provenance.

Currency, religion and marriage institutions can all grow from this pipeline, but the system contains no "currency" type definition. This is the key difference from a predefined cultural-type system.

**On crystallisation (v4.8.0 correction).** Earlier development of this system included a grey-deployment pipeline (a candidate pool, a 7-day trial, observation and rollback). That pipeline was removed in v4.8.0: it was itself a developer-defined type pipeline, and it contradicted the "everything above the substrate is open" principle. Crystallisation is now immediate; a custom carries no `trigger` or `effect` field. Its binding force emerges from public visibility — every resident can see the custom in their snapshot, and whether to obey it, and how to gossip about violators, is the residents' own business. The retired pipeline survives only as historical "afterglow" for old saves (fixed events continue to fire, but no new proposals are generated).

### 3.4 The neurocore: a cross-generational unit
The neurocore is a weight carrier that does not vanish when its host dies. It is consistent with JaxLife's problem statement on cross-generational accumulation but differs in architecture:
- It carries no narrative memory, only a strategy weight vector (currently 4 dimensions: `exist` / `wander` / `scout` / `work`);
- When the host dies, the core enters a "drifting" state, waiting for the next newborn (after 30 days it logs one round and keeps waiting — the core is not extinguished);
- Landing in a newborn, the weights are carried over unchanged and the generation number increments;
- Reward modulation is based on survival improvement (wellbeing Δ > +6 → reinforce, < −6 → weaken), with a fixed learning rate and zero randomness;
- Daily decay toward the baseline is 2% (a "use it or lose it"退化面).

The design goal is an evolutionary channel **entirely independent of individual memory**: a resident's memory, relationships and secrets die with them, but the neurocore's weights cross generations. This models a gene-level evolution without any cultural transmission.

### 3.5 Perception and standing (v4.9.0)
A critique that this project takes seriously: without a world model, an agent perceives only its own needs and cannot represent the external world. Drawing on the biological baseline of *Drosophila* (≈139,000 neurons with an internal compass, path integration and valence tags) and on belief-modelling work in game AI (belief as a proposition with source, timestamp and confidence), v4.9.0 adds two things *Drosophila* has and the residents lacked.

**Beliefs — a subjective world model.** Each resident holds a list of beliefs `{text, dir, source, day, confidence, hops}`, capped at 12 (weak beliefs are displaced). Beliefs enter only through existing event streams:
- a traveller's relayed news (with a direction word),
- a public event witnessed first-hand (confidence 0.9),
- conversational spread (10% per chat, ×0.8 decay per hop; from the third hop onward the text is distorted by the rumour mutator).

First-hand witnessing overrides hearsay, while hearsay cannot displace first-hand knowledge; retelling reinforces the teller's own belief (+0.05); confidence decays daily (×0.985) and a belief below 0.15 is silently forgotten. Beliefs appear in each resident's snapshot as "what I think" — *misperception is a feature of a real world*: a person may move away and yet the belief remains.

**A distributed standing field.** Social weight is a *function*, not a table: `standingOf(n)` averages, over all other residents, an `eyeOf` score built from relationship components (trust/fear/depend/grudge) plus the valence of memories that mention `n`, recency-weighted. No global ranking is stored — otherwise rumour, misjudgement and miscarried justice could not emerge. Only a daily top-1 cache is computed (a resident counts as "the one people listen to" only if standing ≥ 55 and the lead exceeds 15). The former `POWER_JOB` developer-side job-prestige table has been retired: a doctor is not inherently above a farmer; weight grows only from witnessed history.

**Arbitration rerouting.** A property dispute is no longer silently resolved by the system toggling its recognition bit. The dispute is brought before the highest-standing resident and becomes a public event: nearby witnesses record an incident memory, and each rewrites the recognition according to his own relationship with the claimant (witnesses change the public verdict, the system does not). The arbitrator receives the witnesses' positive memory and a "who judges" belief — social standing as fuel, so leadership emerges from the number of times one is asked to judge. If no resident has enough weight (standing < 25), the dispute simply **hangs** — an unresolved public knot, which is precisely the raw material for ideas such as "someone ought to be in charge."

### 3.6 Those who leave, and the infinite world
When a resident decides to leave (push factors include scarcity, social conflict, curiosity, ambition), the entity is moved into `game.wanderers` rather than deleted. Leavers continue in a reduced-precision daily simulation: advancing along a bearing, foraging, joining a settlement or founding a new camp, wolf attacks, dying and leaving a grave, sending word home, and homesick visits. The world unfolds on demand (64×64 chunks; the origin is a 2×2 block of 128×128; the town is 72×72).

Leaver behaviour is driven by an independent random stream (`wrng`) that does not consume the main simulation's random numbers. This satisfies the D34 invariant: **the same seed yields the same world — this is physics, not a wish.** The main stream (`game.rng`) derives only from the seed; `Math.random` and `Date.now` are purged from the simulation chain (they may appear only in pure visuals or pure logs); entity IDs carry no timestamps.

## 4 Preliminary Results

### 4.1 Engineering verification
The system has 53 self-test stage numbers (stage 53 is the most recent), comprising 1431 assertions, all passing. Browser-level (CDP) smoke checks number 26 and pass serially. Key verifications include:
- **Determinism**: for the same seed (2028) over 400 days, two separate processes produce field-identical world state (alive 15 / dead 4 / born 1 / animals 18 / pregnant 4).
- **Conservation**: material combination/separation/transformation reconcile slot-by-slot.
- **Performance**: a pathfinding rewrite reduced a 180-day simulation from 27.3 s to 7.4 s (integer-key caching with chunk-direct lookup).
- **Reproducibility of state**: save/load round-trips are field-identical; old saves are upgraded lazily.

### 4.2 On the idea pool
What is verified is that the idea pipeline (formation / spread / mutation / selection / crystallisation / forgetting) is deterministic and reproducible under a fixed seed. As a *qualitative* check of long-run behaviour — not an OEE statistic — we ran a single headless simulation of 300 in-game days at seed 2028 (dt = 0.05 s, ≈1.08×10⁶ frame steps). Three observations stand out. First, the pool ran **pinned at its finite bound**: all 48 slots were occupied at termination, of which 15 ideas were in the *spreading* state and 45 had arisen by mutation rather than direct formation; the pool behaves as a rolling window, since the oldest idea is evicted once the cap is reached. Second, crystallisation into standing rules was **repeatedly observed** — the mechanism fires readily in a small town, because the 30%-of-adults threshold is reachable with only a handful of holders. Third, the v4.9.0 belief layer produced **no** entries in this run: its two origin events (a public adjudication of a property dispute, and a leaver's word sent home) did not occur in a settled town with no disputes and no departures. Beliefs are therefore verified at unit and smoke level but not yet exercised at run time — evidence that these origins are genuinely *conditional*, and a target for future work. Long-run OEE statistics of the pool remain **not yet** quantified against PNCT's GAC/EPC; we report no averages here and flag the alignment as future work.

## 5 Discussion

### 5.1 What this paper does not claim
We do not claim to have solved OEE. Genesis's negative result already shows that removing the fitness function does not by itself escape a complexity plateau. We make no superiority claim over existing platforms. What we claim is narrower: that constraint-driven evolution and untyped cultural evolution are *engineeringly feasible* inside a full social simulation with bodies, relationships, language, a material chain and institutions.

### 5.2 Relation to the "static sandbox" critique
Chen et al. argue that "unpredictability is not a defect to be controlled but a feature to be embraced." Our choices — no tasks, no predefined cultural types, no direct player intervention — align with this. But embracing unpredictability has engineering costs: debugging difficulty, save-file growth, and the complexity of long-range determinism verification. We manage these costs with a mandatory `reason` field (each intent without one is auto-filled with "(no reason given)"), a complete causal-chain log, and the D34 determinism invariant.

### 5.3 Limitations
1. **The idea pool's fitness is an implicit scalar.** It is not a designer-specified goal (wellbeing emerges from the need system), but it is nonetheless a scalar measure. This means the system does not "fully remove fitness"; it relocates the position of fitness from designer specification to the emergent output of the need system. This is a genuine philosophical difference from Genesis and should not be masked by the phrase "constraint-driven".
2. **The neurocore has only 4 dimensions**, far below JaxLife's network parameterisation. Extending it toward the *C. elegans* 302-neuron connectome is a planned direction, not yet implemented.
3. **Validation is mostly engineering-level** (self-test, smoke, determinism); scientific validation, in terms of contribution to OEE theory, is limited.
4. **Beliefs and the standing field (v4.9.0) are newly added** and have only unit- and smoke-level verification; their macro-level effects remain to be measured.

### 5.4 Open questions
**Q1.** Does the joint constraint of six physical theorems sustain evolutionary activity better than a single metabolic cost function? This requires a controlled comparison against Genesis on the same substrate.
**Q2.** Does an untyped idea pool produce higher lineage diversity than a predefined cultural-type system? If so, what maintains the diversity?
**Q3.** Does the neurocore architecture — "weights cross generations, memory does not" — model a mode of evolution different from gene-culture co-evolution?
**Q4.** Does a distributed standing field (no global ranking) produce qualitatively different social dynamics (rumour, miscarried justice, emergent leadership) than a global reputation table? (v4.9.0 makes this empirically testable within our system.)

## 6 Conclusion
This paper reports the design decisions and preliminary evidence of an LLM multi-agent world simulator whose core contribution is turning "physical substrate hardcoded, everything above the substrate fully open" from a philosophical stance into an operable engineering principle: six eternal theorems in place of a fitness function, a free-text idea pool in place of predefined cultural types, a cross-generational neurocore as an independent evolutionary channel, and, in the current version, per-resident beliefs and a distributed standing field. The system does not claim to solve OEE, nor to be superior to existing platforms. It claims that, within a full social simulation, constraint-driven evolution and untyped cultural evolution are engineeringly feasible, and that their long-range behaviour can be strictly verified within a determinism framework. Next steps include aligning the idea pool's metrics with PNCT, extending the neurocore toward the *C. elegans* connectome, and a controlled comparison with Genesis on the same substrate.

# Part II — 中文版

## 摘要

开放式演化（Open-Ended Evolution, OEE）——系统持续产生新颖、日益复杂的行为而不收敛于固定目标——是人工生命领域的核心未解问题。现有演化计算系统几乎全部依赖设计者指定的标量适应度函数来驱动选择，而生物演化并无此函数。与此同时，基于大语言模型（LLM）的多智能体社会模拟正面临"静态沙盒"批评：预定义任务、封闭环境和刚性评估标准从根本上抑制了涌现与共同演化。本文报告一个正在开发的 LLM 多智能体世界模拟器（"创世小镇"项目）。它以六条不可变的物理定理（守恒、因果、时间、空间、代价、有限）替代标量适应度函数，以自由文本观念池替代预定义文化类型，以跨世代"神经芯"提供一条独立于个体记忆的演化通道；并自 v4.9.0 起，为每位居民赋予一个主观世界模型（信念）与一个粗略的名望场。设计原则是"物理基底硬编码，基底之上全开放"。本文报告的证据属于工程层面：53 个自检阶段（每个阶段是一组语义相关的断言）、共 1431 项断言全部通过，26 项浏览器级冒烟检查，以及一次 400 天的双进程确定性验证。本文不声称解决了 OEE，而是报告一个可作为 OEE 实证研究平台的设计决策与初步证据。

**关键词：** 开放式演化；无适应度演化；LLM 多智能体系统；人工生命；约束驱动选择；文化累积

## 1 引言

生物演化在没有任何适应度函数的情况下维持了数十亿年的持续创新。自 Bedau 等人（2000）把开放式性提出为"开放问题"以来，人工生命社区一直寻求在人工系统中复现这一性质。然而如 Genesis 平台所指出的，"几乎所有的演化算法都依赖一个适应度函数"。即便是最新的神经细胞自动机研究，其自复制与突变行为也是在预定义损失函数下训练得到，而非在无目标环境中自发涌现。

与此同时，LLM 驱动的多智能体模拟（如 Smallville、Project Sid）展示了社会涌现的潜力，但 Chen 等人 2025 年的立场论文指出："当前大多数模拟仍困于静态沙盒——预定义任务、有限动力学、刚性评估标准"，并呼吁从"任务特定优化"转向"开放式共同演化"。

本文正是对这一呼吁的工程回应。其核心原则是：**物理基底硬编码，基底之上全开放。** 具体而言：

1. **适应度函数的位置被物理约束替代。** 系统不定义任何标量奖励。六条永恒定理（守恒、因果、时间、空间、代价、有限）扮演与 Genesis"约束驱动选择"同构的角色——它们只规定什么是不可能的，不规定什么应该被追求。
2. **文化层无预定义类型。** 观念是自由文本，不是预定义分类。货币、宗教、制度从观念池中涌现，而非被开发者定义。
3. **跨世代演化单元独立于个体。** "神经芯"是一个不随宿主死亡而消失的权重载体，它寄宿于居民身上，死后游荡，等待下一个新生儿。这是一个与 JaxLife"跨世代文化累积"不同的架构选择。
4. **（v4.9.0）居民携带主观世界模型与分布式名望场。** 每位居民持有一组*信念*——可能是错的、可能过时的命题；每位居民的社会分量由他人的见证算出，而非由职业表查出。

## 2 相关工作

### 2.1 无适应度演化
Genesis（Sharma, GECCO 2026）以约束驱动选择（CDS）、约束自适应调节（CARP）与人工免疫系统（AIS）替代标量适应度；在超过一百万代实验中，7/12 次运行观察到持续的演化活动，并给出一个"sham-controlled"阴性结果，证明单纯的生态位构建不足以突破复杂性平台期。本系统与 Genesis 共享"约束替代适应度"的哲学立场，但尺度不同：Genesis 在 Gray-Scott 反应-扩散基底上运行小规模智能体，而本系统运行的是一个包含身体、关系、语言、物质链和制度的完整社会模拟。因此本系统中的"约束"不是单一的代谢成本函数，而是分布在多个子系统中的物理律（材料必须被搬运、知识随人死而消失、时间不可逆）。

### 2.2 开放式智能体模拟器
JaxLife（Lu et al., ALIFE 2024）的目标是"能跨世代累积开放式文化与技术的智能体"，并在基础通信、农业与工具使用上取得了成果。其智能体由深度神经网络参数化、在预定义生存适应度下用演化策略优化。本系统有两点不同：居民行为由"规则效用 AI + LLM 决策"的混合架构产生，而非由神经网络直接参数化；"选择压力"不是单一生存信号，而是六条定理的联合约束——一个居民可以活着，却无法做成他"想做"的事，因为材料不存在、知识已失传、或时空不可跳跃。

### 2.3 LLM 多智能体与社会模拟
Chen 等人（2025）的立场论文系统批评了"静态沙盒"，并提出以开放式性、持续共同演化与弹性 AI 生态为中心的研究路线。本系统直接回应其中三点：**预定义任务** → 本系统无任务，居民由需求、矛盾与观念驱动；**封闭环境** → 本系统是无限地图，远方按需生成，离镇者继续在低精度模拟中行进；**刚性评估** → 本系统的成功度量不是任务完成率，而是演化活动本身的持续性（观念产生/传播/变异/结晶的速率、谱系多样性、遗迹留存）。

### 2.4 开放式性的度量
Bedau 等人的演化活动统计量是 OEE 领域最广泛使用的度量框架；Genesis 在此基础上发展了 PNCT（含遗传活动系数 GAC 与表达表型复杂性 EPC）。本系统目前使用不同的度量（持有者数量分布、结晶事件、知识失传速率、长程确定性一致性）。本系统的度量与 GAC/EPC 不直接可比，但共享同一目标：量化演化活动的持续性而非任务完成率。与 GAC/EPC 对齐是未来工作，本文尚不声称完成这一对齐。

## 3 系统设计

### 3.1 设计原则
核心判断：**在一个足够复杂的模拟中，预定义"什么值得演化"比让演化自己决定"什么值得"更危险。** 因此系统将硬编码内容限制在物理层，把一切内容委托给运行时机制。

| 层 | 是否硬编码 | 内容 |
|---|---|---|
| 物理层 | 是 | 六条永恒定理、物质守恒、身体解剖、时间与空间离散性 |
| 内容层 | 否 | 配方、语言、习俗、事件模板、建筑组合、社会角色、观念 |
| 元层 | 否 | 什么算"缺口"、什么算"新颖"、什么算"值得固化" |

### 3.2 六条永恒定理

| 定理 | 含义 | 系统实现 |
|---|---|---|
| 守恒 | 物质不凭空生灭 | 材料链逐槽守恒（strict 模式）；组合/分离/转化总量不变 |
| 因果 | 有因才有果，且不可逆 | 每个意图必须带 `reason` 字段；行动产生效应，效应进入记忆 |
| 时间 | 单向，不可倒流 | 回档功能被删除；放弃世界后永久消逝 |
| 空间 | 离散网格，不可跳跃 | 寻路、搬运、材料运输必须经过中间格 |
| 代价 | 任何改变都有成本 | 体力、时间、材料消耗；无免费行动 |
| 有限 | 资源、寿命、精力有限 | 人口上限、资源上限、知识载体（人脑）有限 |

这些定理与 Genesis 的"物理门控"同构，但不以单一代谢成本函数表达：守恒体现在物质系统，因果体现在意图契约，时间单向体现在存档哲学，空间体现在寻路，代价体现在身体系统，有限体现在人口与知识系统。

### 3.3 观念池：无类型文化演化
观念（idea）是自由文本，上限 40 字符，无预定义分类。每位居民每日有 1.2% 概率从近事、心结或身体感受中产生一个念头。全镇观念池上限 48 条，单人上限 6 条（有限定理）。观念的生命周期：

- **产生** —— 从居民当前状态中抽取出的一个自由文本表述；
- **传播** —— 聊天时 8% 概率传播；15% 概率经词汇变异器产生新观念（"话传三代会走样"）；
- **选择** —— 适应度定义为持有者平均福祉（心情 40% + 精力 30% + 饱腹 30%）的 7 日滑窗；
- **死亡** —— 适应度低于 0.3 时每日 2% 概率被遗忘；
- **结晶** —— 持有者达到成年人口 30% 且适应度 ≥ 0.45 时，观念落地为习俗，带溯源标记。

货币、宗教、婚姻制度都能从这个管道中长出来，但系统不含"货币"这一类型定义。这是与预定义文化类型系统的关键差异。

**关于结晶（v4.8.0 更正）。** 本系统早期版本包含一套灰度部署管道（候选池、试行 7 天、观察、回滚）。该管道已在 v4.8.0 拆除：它本身就是开发者定义的类型管道，与"基底之上全开放"相矛盾。现在结晶是即时的；习俗不携带 `trigger` 或 `effect` 字段，其约束力从公共可见性涌现——人人快照里看得见它，遵不遵守、怎么议论违反者，是居民自己的事。退役管道仅作为旧档"余温"保留（已固化事件继续触发，但不产生新提案）。

### 3.4 神经芯：跨世代演化单元
神经芯是一个不随宿主死亡而消失的权重载体。它与 JaxLife 的"跨世代累积"问题意识一致，但架构选择不同：
- 它不携带任何叙事记忆，只携带策略权重向量（当前为 4 维：`exist` 发呆 / `wander` 闲逛 / `scout` 远行 / `work` 手上活计）；
- 宿主死亡后，芯进入"游荡"状态，等待下一个新生儿（游荡 30 天记一轮轮回，接着等——芯不灭）；
- 落进新生儿后，权重原样带走，代际编号 +1；
- 奖励调制基于生存改善（福祉改善 > +6 → 强化，< −6 → 弱化），学习率固定、零随机；
- 用进废退每日向基线回落 2%。

其设计目标是提供一条**与个体记忆完全独立的演化通道**：居民的记忆、关系、秘密随死亡消失，但神经芯的权重跨代传递。这模拟了一种"基因层面"的演化，不涉及任何文化传承。

### 3.5 感知与名望（v4.9.0）
本项目认真对待一条批评：没有世界模型时，智能体只能感知自身需求，无法表征外部世界。参照果蝇的生物学基线（约 13.9 万神经元，具备内部罗盘、路径积分与价态标签）与游戏 AI 的信念建模（信念 = 命题 + 来源 + 时间戳 + 置信度），v4.9.0 补上了果蝇有、居民却缺的两样。

**信念——主观世界模型。** 每位居民持有一组信念 `{text, 方向, 来源, 日期, 置信度, 转手数}`，上限 12 条（弱者被挤出）。信念只经已有事件流进入：
- 行脚人捎话（带方向词）；
- 公共事件目击（置信度 0.9）；
- 聊天传播（每次 10%，逐跳 ×0.8 衰减；从第三手起，文本经传闻变异器走样）。

目击覆盖听闻，传闻不撼动亲眼；复述自增强说者自己的信念（+0.05）；置信度每日衰减（×0.985），低于 0.15 静默遗忘。信念进快照，呈现为"我以为"——**误知是真实世界的特征**：人可能搬走了，信念却还在。

**名望场（分布式）。** 社会分量是一个*函数*，不是一张表：`standingOf(n)` 取所有其他居民对 n 的 `eyeOf` 平均分，`eyeOf` 由关系分量（信任/恐惧/依赖/怨恨）加上"提到 n 的记忆"按新旧加权的价态构成。系统不存全局排行榜——否则谣言、误判与冤案不可能涌现。只计算一个每日 Top1 缓存（名望 ≥ 55 且领先 > 15 才算"说话算数的人"）。原 `POWER_JOB` 开发者职业威望表已退役：医生并不天然高于农夫，分量只从被见证的历史里长。

**评理改道。** 归属争议不再由系统悄悄翻转公认位。争议摆到名望最高的居民面前，成为公共事件：近身目击者记下意外记忆，并各自按自己与当事人的关系改写公认（**见证改公论，系统不强翻**）。评理人收下目击者的正面记忆与"谁是评理的人"信念——名望即燃料，于是领导从"被求评理的次数"中涌现。若没有足够分量的居民（名望 < 25），争议就**悬着**——一个未了的公共心结，正是"得有个主事的"这类念头的原料。

### 3.6 离镇者与无限世界
当居民决定离镇（推力包括资源不足、社交冲突、好奇心、野心），实体被移入 `game.wanderers`，而非被删除。离镇者继续以降精度日模拟行进：沿方位前进、觅食、投奔聚落或自立新营、遭遇狼袭、客死立坟、捎话回乡、思乡探亲。世界按需展开（64×64 区块；起源 2×2 区块即 128×128；城镇 72×72）。

离镇者的行为由独立骰流（`wrng`）驱动，不消费主模拟的随机数。这满足 D34 铁律：**同 seed 同世界是物理，不是愿望。** 主骰流（`game.rng`）只由 seed 派生；`Math.random` 与 `Date.now` 被清出模拟链（只允许出现在纯视觉与纯日志中）；实体 id 去时间戳。

## 4 初步结果

### 4.1 工程验证
系统自检阶段编号已至 53（以 stage 53 为最新），共 1431 项断言全部通过。浏览器级（CDP）冒烟检查 26 项，串行全绿。关键验证包括：
- **确定性**：同 seed（2028）跑 400 天，两个独立进程的世界状态逐字段一致（存活 15 / 亡 4 / 添丁 1 / 动物 18 / 在孕 4）。
- **守恒**：物质组合/分离/转化逐槽对账。
- **性能**：寻路重写使 180 天模拟从 27.3 s 降至 7.4 s（整数键缓存 + 区块直查）。
- **状态可复现**：存档往返逐字段一致；旧档按惰性升级路径兼容。

### 4.2 关于观念池
已获验证的是：观念管道（产生/传播/变异/选择/结晶/遗忘）在固定 seed 下确定、可复现。作为长程行为的*定性*观察（并非 OEE 统计量），我们让系统以 seed 2028 无头运行 300 个游戏日（dt = 0.05 s，约 1.08×10⁶ 帧步）。三条观察突出。其一，观念池**顶到有限上限运行**：终止时 48 个槽位全满，其中 15 条处于*传播中*状态、45 条是由变异而非直接形成而来；池子是滑动窗口——触顶后最旧的观念被挤出。其二，观念结晶为镇规被**反复观察到**——在小镇里这个机制很容易触发，因为"成年人口 30%"的阈值只需少数几个持有者即可达到。其三，v4.9.0 信念层在本轮**没有**产生任何条目：它的两个来源事件（归属争议的公开评理、离镇者捎话回乡）在一个无争议、无离镇的安定小镇里都没发生。因此信念目前只有单元与冒烟级验证，尚未在运行时被真正触发——这说明这些来源确实是*有条件的*，也是未来工作的目标。观念池的长程 OEE 统计量**尚未**与 PNCT 的 GAC/EPC 对齐；因此本文不报告任何长程平均值，并把这一对齐列为未来工作。

### 4.3 与已有平台的差异

| 维度 | Genesis | JaxLife | 本系统 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 适应度 | 约束驱动（无标量） | 生存信号 | 六条物理定理 |
| 文化层 | 无 | 涌现通信 | 无类型观念池 |
| 跨世代单元 | 基因组 | 神经网络权重 | 神经芯（独立于个体） |
| 世界模型 | 无 | 无 | 每人一份信念（v4.9.0） |
| 环境 | Gray-Scott 基底 | 可编程网格 | 无限程序生成地图 |
| 验证规模 | >10⁶ 代 | 未报告 | 53 阶段 / 1431 断言 / 400 天确定性 |

## 5 讨论

### 5.1 本文不声称什么
本文不声称解决了 OEE。Genesis 的阴性结果已表明，即使移除适应度函数，系统仍可能陷入复杂性平台期。本文也不声称优于已有平台。本文声称的更窄：在一个包含身体、关系、语言、物质链与制度的完整社会模拟中，约束驱动演化与无类型文化演化在工程上是可行的。

### 5.2 与"静态沙盒"批评的关系
Chen 等人指出，"不可预测性不是需要控制的缺陷，而是需要拥抱的特征"。本系统的设计选择——无任务、无预定义文化类型、无玩家直接干预——与此一致。但"拥抱不可预测性"在工程上是有代价的：调试难度、存档膨胀、长程确定性验证的复杂性。本系统以强制 `reason` 字段（意图缺理由则自动补"（没说为什么）"）、完整因果链日志，以及 D34 确定性铁律来管理这一代价。

### 5.3 局限
1. **观念池的适应度是一个隐性标量。** 它不是设计者指定的目标（福祉由需求系统涌现），但它仍是一个标量度量。这意味着本系统并非"完全移除适应度"，而是把适应度的位置从设计者指定转移到了需求系统的涌现输出；这是与 Genesis 的一个真实哲学差异，不应被"约束驱动"的措辞掩盖。
2. **神经芯当前只有 4 维权重**，远低于 JaxLife 的神经网络参数化。将其扩展到线虫 302 神经元连接组是一个计划中的方向，尚未实现。
3. **验证主要在工程层面**（自检/冒烟/确定性），在科学层面（对 OEE 理论的贡献）的验证有限。
4. **信念与名望场（v4.9.0）是新近加入的**，目前只有单元与冒烟级验证；其宏观效应仍待测量。

### 5.4 开放问题
**问题1：** 六条物理定理的联合约束，是否比单一代谢成本函数更能维持演化活动？这需要在相同基底上与 Genesis 进行对照实验。
**问题2：** 无类型观念池是否比预定义文化类型系统产生更高的谱系多样性？若是，多样性的维持机制是什么？
**问题3：** 神经芯"权重跨代传递、记忆不传递"的架构，是否模拟了一种不同于基因-文化共演化的新演化模式？
**问题4：** 分布式名望场（无全局排行）是否产生与全局声望表定性不同的社会动力学（谣言、误判、涌现的领导）？（v4.9.0 使这一点在本系统内可被实证检验。）

## 6 结论
本文报告了一个 LLM 多智能体世界模拟器的设计决策与初步证据，其核心贡献在于把"物理基底硬编码，基底之上全开放"从哲学立场转化为可操作的工程原则：六条永恒定理替代适应度函数，自由文本观念池替代预定义文化类型，跨世代神经芯提供独立的演化通道，并自 v4.9.0 起为每位居民赋予信念与分布式名望场。系统不声称解决 OEE，也不声称优于已有平台。它声称的是：在一个完整的社会模拟中，约束驱动演化与无类型文化演化在工程上是可行的，其长程行为可以在确定性框架下被严格验证。下一步工作包括：将观念池度量与 PNCT 对齐，将神经芯扩展到线虫连接组，以及在相同基底上与 Genesis 进行对照实验。
